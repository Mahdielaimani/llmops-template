Labels:

- `relevant` — the answer addresses the question asked. A clear statement that
  the sources do not contain the answer (`insufficient_evidence`) is relevant.
- `partially_relevant` — the answer addresses a related but different question,
  or buries the asked-for fact in unrelated material.
- `irrelevant` — the answer does not address the question.
